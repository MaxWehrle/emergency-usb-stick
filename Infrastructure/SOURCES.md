## State, tribal, territorial, county, and city information

The sources above support a **national baseline**, not a specific local plan. Copy `My-Files/Local-Info/LOCAL-INFO-TEMPLATE.txt` to `My-Files/Local-Info/local-info.txt` **on your personal copy only**, then enter your local emergency management agency, evacuation zone, shelter arrangements, water utility, and NOAA Weather Radio station. Do not commit filled-in details to a public branch. Local warnings and orders take priority over these cards.

## Files supplied by this project

`MANIFEST.sha256` lists the SHA-256 checksum of each packaged file except itself. The ZIP checksum is stated on the project homepage. The GitHub branch archive is generated dynamically and does not have the same checksum as the standalone release ZIP.

To keep an official publication offline for **personal use**, download it yourself from the publisher and place it in `My-Files/Documents/`. Check current rights and dates before passing copies to others.
