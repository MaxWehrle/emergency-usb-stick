# Flood and flash flood

Water depth and road damage are hard to judge. Move to safer ground.

## What you can do

1. Follow local flood warnings and evacuation orders. Move to higher ground when flash flooding threatens.
2. Do not walk or drive into floodwater, and never drive around a road barrier.
3. Avoid flooded basements and electrical equipment. Turn off power only if it can be done safely.
4. Return and clean up only when local officials say the area is safe.
