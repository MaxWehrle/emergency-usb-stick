# Power outage

A power failure can also affect water, heating, phones, stores, and traffic signals.

## What you can do

1. Check whether the outage is limited to your home. Stay away from downed wires and damaged electrical equipment.
2. Use flashlights, a charged power bank, and a battery or hand-crank radio. Save your phone battery for important calls.
3. Never use a generator, charcoal grill, or camp stove indoors. Run generators outside, at least 20 feet from doors, windows, and vents.
4. Follow local utility and emergency instructions. Check refrigerated food safety after the outage.
