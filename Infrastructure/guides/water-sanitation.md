# Water and sanitation

Tap water may be unavailable or unsafe after a disaster.

## What you can do

1. Follow the exact notice from your local water utility or health department. A boil-water notice and a do-not-drink notice are different.
2. Use bottled or otherwise confirmed safe water for drinking, food, brushing teeth, and baby formula when tap water is unsafe.
3. Keep supplies for hand hygiene and sanitation. Plan for people with special medical or infant feeding needs.
4. Do not judge water safety by its appearance or smell. Check local instructions before using tap water again.
