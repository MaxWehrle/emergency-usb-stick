# Wildfire smoke

Smoke can be harmful even far from a fire; local air quality and orders matter.

## What you can do

1. Check current local air quality and emergency orders when service is available. Leave immediately if officials order evacuation.
2. If staying home is safe, reduce smoke entering rooms and use a cleaner-air room or appropriate air cleaner if available.
3. If the home becomes too hot or smoky, seek a safer indoor location. Check on children and people with heart or lung conditions.
4. Do not rely on a cloth mask to filter wildfire smoke.
