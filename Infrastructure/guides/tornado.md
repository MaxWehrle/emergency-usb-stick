# Tornado

A tornado warning for your area means act quickly.

## What you can do

1. Go to a basement, safe room, or small interior room on the lowest floor of a sturdy building, away from windows.
2. Cover your head and neck. Follow the shelter plan at work or school.
3. Mobile homes, tents, and vehicles are unsafe in a tornado. Reach a sturdy shelter if you can do so before it arrives.
4. Keep listening to local alerts and NOAA Weather Radio if available.
