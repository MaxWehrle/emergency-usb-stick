Extreme heat

Find a cooler place, reduce activity, and check on people at higher risk.

What you can do

1. Follow National Weather Service heat alerts for your area. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest hours.
2. Drink water regularly unless a clinician has told you to limit fluids. Find shade or air conditioning.
3. Check on older adults, children, and people with health conditions. Never leave people or pets in a parked car.
4. For severe symptoms such as confusion or loss of consciousness, call 911.
