Basic supplies

Start with a small kit you can actually use and maintain.

What you can do

1. Plan water, food, lights, a radio, chargers, medicines, and hygiene items around your household's needs.
2. A common US planning baseline is at least three days of supplies; longer may be needed in some places or for specific needs.
3. Include children, pets, accessibility needs, and a plan for leaving home quickly.
4. Check dates, batteries, and your backup files regularly.
