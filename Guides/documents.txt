Documents and records

Keep important originals protected and make readable backup copies.

What you can do

1. Collect the IDs, insurance information, medical records, and other documents that matter to your household.
2. Put sensitive digital copies in a tested encrypted vault. Keep its password and recovery key separate from the drive.
3. Store original documents safely; a digital copy may not satisfy a specific agency's requirements.
4. Test opening a backup copy on another device and keep a second encrypted copy elsewhere.
