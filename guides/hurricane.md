# Hurricane and coastal storm

Coastal risks and evacuation zones are local; plan before a storm arrives.

## What to do

1. Check your local evacuation zone and route in advance. Follow local officials and leave immediately if ordered.
2. Monitor National Weather Service and local emergency management information when available.
3. Keep a go-bag with medicines, documents, water, and chargers ready. Protect your property only when it is safe to do so.
4. If told to stay, use an interior room away from windows. Return after evacuation only when officials say it is safe.
