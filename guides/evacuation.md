# Evacuation

An evacuation route or shelter can change. Current local instructions come first.

## What to do

1. If local officials tell you to leave, follow their route and timing. Do not wait for this offline card to update.
2. Take needed medicine, ID, a small document folder, phone, charger, water, sturdy shoes, and supplies for children or pets if you can do so safely.
3. Tell a trusted contact where you plan to go. Help neighbors who need assistance without placing yourself in danger.
4. Return only when local officials say it is safe.
