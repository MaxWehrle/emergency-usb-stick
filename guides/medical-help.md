# Medical help and first aid

Call for emergency help, protect yourself, and use skills you have practiced.

## What to do

1. Make sure the scene is safe for you before helping someone else.
2. For an immediate police, fire, or medical emergency, call 911. Give your location and describe what happened. Stay on the line until the call-taker tells you to hang up.
3. Give first aid within your training while waiting for responders. Keep a list of medicines and medical needs ready for your own household.
4. Refresh first-aid training; a short card cannot replace hands-on practice.
